I'm an **AI Research Scientist** and a PhD candidate in Deep Learning and Astrophysics at the **University of Geneva** and **FHNW**. On **1 December 2026** I'm joining **Sony AI** in Zurich as a Research Scientist in the AI for Chip Design team.

I work on **scientific machine learning**: diffusion models, transformers for PDE surrogates, video generation, and long-horizon spatio-temporal forecasting. I spent my PhD teaching generative models to understand the Sun, with work on solar image synthesis, super-resolution of magnetograms and forecasting of solar evolution. Since then I've scaled this work up to foundation models for science at Polymathic AI and to semiconductor device simulation at Sony AI.

Before machine learning, my background was in mathematics and astrophysics. I care about models that are accurate, physically consistent and interpretable. On this site you'll find my work, slides, code and notes. Feel free to reach out!
