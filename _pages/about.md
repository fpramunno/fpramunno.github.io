Bio
======
Hello, my name is Francesco Pio Ramunno. I am an AI Research Scientist and a PhD candidate in Deep Learning and Astrophysics at the University of Geneva (Switzerland), in collaboration with the University of Applied Sciences and Arts Northwestern Switzerland (FHNW), supervised by Prof. Slava Voloshynovskiy and Prof. André Csillaghy. From **1 December 2026** I will join **Sony AI** in Zurich as an AI Research Scientist in the AI for Chip Design team.

My research is in scientific machine learning: generative models (diffusion models in particular), transformers for PDE surrogate modeling, video generation, and long-horizon spatio-temporal forecasting. During my PhD I applied these methods to real-world data from the Sun, using DDPMs and latent diffusion models for solar image synthesis, super-resolution and forecasting. More recently I have worked on large-scale scientific foundation models at Polymathic AI and on scalable transformer architectures for semiconductor device simulation at Sony AI.

I am especially interested in the theoretical foundations of these models and in making them work across scientific domains. I want AI tools that are accurate, physically consistent and interpretable.

On this site I share updates on my research, including slides, code and publications. Feel free to reach out by email if you'd like to connect or collaborate!

Sony AI — Research Scientist Intern in Machine Learning
------

📍 Zurich (Switzerland) | 🗓️ February 2026 – August 2026

I designed and implemented a scalable dual-attention transformer for PDE surrogate modeling on large semiconductor meshes (500k+ nodes). It combines global token attention with adaptive sparse local refinement. The model improved the state of the art by more than 30% on open-source 2D/3D PDE benchmarks and on Sony's in-house semiconductor simulation datasets, and it is now part of Sony AI's production semiconductor simulation framework. The resulting paper (first author) has been submitted to ICLR 2027.

Polymathic AI (Simons Foundation) — AI Foundation Models for Science Intern
------

📍 New York City (USA) | 🗓️ March 2025 – August 2025

Supervised by Rudy Morel and David Fouhey. I built and scaled data pipelines for a 100+ TB multimodal spatio-temporal NASA solar dataset, synchronizing 14 modalities through temporal alignment, spatial reprojection and preprocessing. I designed diffusion-based video models for long-horizon scientific forecasting that reduce exposure bias and are more temporally stable than autoregressive baselines; this work was published at **NeurIPS 2025 (Main Track)**. I also scaled high-resolution multi-channel diffusion models to distributed training on up to 32 NVIDIA H200 GPUs.

European Space Agency (ESA) — Data Science and Machine Learning Intern
------

The reproducible workflows and documentation were delivered to the ESA Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre (NEOCC).
